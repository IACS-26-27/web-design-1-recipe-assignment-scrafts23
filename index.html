Lasagna

Ingredients

  • Homemade sauce w/meatballs and sausage made in advance
  • Lasagna pasta (around one box)
  • Ricotta cheese (8 oz container)
  • 1 egg
  • Provolone cheese slices
  • (Optional) parm cheese topping

Step One

Preheat oven to 350

Step Two

Boil a pot of water to cook the pasta then cook per box instructions. Set aside and let cool when done

Step Three

Separate the meat from sauce and put meat in a separate bowl. Cut sausage into small pieces and smash up meatballs. Set aside. Mix in one egg thoroughly to the container of ricotta cheese. Set aside

Step Four

Grab tin and put thin layer of sauce to bottom of tin

Step Five

Put a layer of pasta slightly overlapping to cover the whole pan. Followed by dollops of cheese don’t spread the cheese but space out blobs. In between blobs of cheese place blobs of meat. Then drizzle sauce overtop, and layer more pasta

Step Six

Repeat the process of cheese, meat, sauce then pasta until it reaches the top of the pan or you run out of pasta. Make sure to not place cheese in the same spot on multiple layers. When you reach the top one more layer of sauce then place provolone slices in a layer on top.

Step Seven

Cook for ~45 minutes at 350. Sprinkle it with parmesan cheese then enjoy.
